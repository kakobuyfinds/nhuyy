Before approving warehouse QC

  1. Save the listing link and the exact color and size you selected. Check the return window before placing the order.
  2. For shoes, compare measurements taken the same way on a pair you own. For tops, lay a garment flat and compare chest width, body length and sleeve length in centimeters.
  3. When warehouse photos are available, match the label and color to your order. Ask for a ruler photo if a measurement is unclear, and check the areas you expect to wear hardest, such as outsoles, seams or zippers.

Estimate the total in USD

Add the item, local delivery and agent fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Use one currency for all figures and check which charges your shipping quote already includes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a browsable list of product finds that helps you shortlist items before ordering with a shopping agent. Use the product page to verify the seller link and available variants.

Why can shipping quotes differ for the same items?

Carriers can price a parcel using its actual weight or a dimensional weight based on the box size. Compare quotes using the same packed dimensions, destination and included services.
